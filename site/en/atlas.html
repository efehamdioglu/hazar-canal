Legends tell of a being who carries the sky on his shoulders. To him the burden is not weight, but destiny.

At first touch, the golden light of apricot rises. Like the warm trace the sun leaves before it sets. Coconut weaves a creamy layer;

Lily of the valley lies hidden at its heart. A divine purity, an unseen grace.

Then the darkness deepens. Cocoa takes the stage; dense, dark, almost ancient. Amber Xtreme magnifies the power, widens the aura, casts an invisible halo around you.

And sandalwood… Like the memory of the earth. Timeless. Unshakeable. Eternal.

This is a myth.

Woody. Deep. Divine.
The summit of quiet luxury.

“If you love these, Atlas is for you.”

  • If you love people wondering what you're wearing.
  • If you think carrying a dangerous aura is no bad thing.
  • If you love scents that are woody and bold, but never suffocating.

Atlas is for you.
